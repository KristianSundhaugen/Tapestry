## 1. Goal

What outcome does the user want, in one or two sentences? Who benefits and how will they notice?

## 2. Users and usage

Who uses this, how often, and in what situation. Concrete scenarios beat personas.

### Explicitly out of scope

## 4. Constraints

- **Technical**: existing code, languages, frameworks, APIs it must fit with
- **Operational**: hosting, budget, deadlines, team size
- **Compliance / policy**: data handling, licensing, accessibility, regulation

## 5. Tech stack preferences

What the user wants used, what they refuse, and what they are indifferent to. Record "no preference" explicitly so the planner does not have to guess.

## 6. Non-functional requirements

Performance, scale, availability, security, observability, i18n, accessibility. Numbers where possible ("p95 under 300 ms at 50 req/s"), "not a concern at this stage" where honest.

## 7. Success criteria

How the user will judge the result done. Each criterion must be checkable by someone who did not build it.
